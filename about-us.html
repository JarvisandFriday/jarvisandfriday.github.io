Our Engineering Philosophy

Operating from our specialized engineering facility in Ichalkaranji, Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, ARC Suspension combines precision hydraulic damping with advanced composite material science. Rather than utilizing generic, off-the-shelf import components tuned for smooth foreign motorways, every ARC kit is tuned specifically for the suspension geometry and dynamic demands of Indian vehicles.

Two Distinct Suspension Technologies

Twin-Tube Gas Shock Absorbers & Struts

Engineered for modern ladder-frame and monocoque platforms—including Toyota Innova Crysta, Toyota Fortuner, Mahindra Thar (2020+ coil-spring setup), and Mahindra Scorpio N. Our progressive compression and tuned rebound valving eliminate high-speed vehicle float, prevent nose dive under firm braking, and control secondary bounce after speed breakers.

High-Durability Composite Leaf Springs

Pioneered for utility, commercial, and off-road leaf-sprung vehicles (including 1st Gen Thar CRDe and utility platforms). Composite leaf springs deliver massive unsprung weight reduction, exceptional fatigue resistance, and eliminate the sagging, inter-leaf friction, and recambering maintenance of traditional multi-leaf steel packs.

100% Direct Bolt-On Fitment Guarantee

Every ARC suspension upgrade is manufactured to OEM dimensional tolerances. Installation utilizes factory mounting brackets and hardware points with zero cutting, welding, drilling, or structural alterations. Any competent automotive mechanic or wheel alignment centre can execute installation in 2 to 3 hours.
