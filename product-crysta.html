Description

Upgrade your Toyota Innova Crysta's stability, body control, and road holding with ARC Suspension. Specifically designed to address lateral body roll, high-speed float, and speed-breaker bounce on Indian roads, ARC shock absorbers provide composed handling while retaining factory ride height and springs.

Benefits:

  • Increased Stability: Firmer, tuned damping controls body float and keeps the vehicle composed at highway speeds.
  • Reduced Body Roll & Sway: Limits lateral body sway during lane changes, curves, and sudden maneuvers.
  • Reduced Steering Shakiness: Filters harsh vibrations through the chassis before reaching the driver.
  • Enhanced Braking Control: Reduces front nose dive under firm braking, keeping the vehicle balanced.
  • Tyre Life Protection: Reduces uneven caster and camber wear caused by uncontrolled vertical movement.
  • Tuned for Indian Road Conditions: Calibrated specifically to withstand potholes, speed breakers, and highway undulations.

How Do I Get ARC Suspension Installed?

  • Direct Replacement: Any qualified local garage, workshop, or tyre alignment centre can install this kit. If they can replace stock shock absorbers, they can install ARC dampers.
  • 100% Direct Bolt-On: Mounts directly onto OEM mounting brackets with zero cutting, welding, or chassis modification.
  • High-Durability Bushings Included: Kit comes with high-quality mounting bushings so installers have all necessary mounting hardware.
  • Factory Technical Support: Direct telephone assistance (+91 9922099292 / +91 9422047019) is available if any installer needs fitment guidance.

Shock Absorbers: 40,000 km or 1 Year (whichever occurs first) against manufacturing or damping defects.
