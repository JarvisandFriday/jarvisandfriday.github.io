Damping Dynamics & Hydraulic Tuning

The Toyota Innova Crysta is praised for passenger space, but its factory suspension calibration allows noticeable lateral body roll during highway lane changes and sustained bounce after encountering speed breakers.

Rebound Damping Control (+30%)

By increasing rebound resistance, ARC shocks prevent the rear axle from springing upward abruptly after speed breakers, eliminating secondary passenger bounce.

Braking Nose Dive Mitigation

Calibrated front compression damping resists forward weight transfer under heavy emergency braking, keeping the vehicle level and reducing braking distance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this ARC kit fit the 2023–2026+ Innova Crysta?

Yes. The identical standard ARC kit fits all Innova Crysta models from the 2016 launch generation through currently produced Crysta variants.

Q: Does this kit alter the vehicle's ground clearance or lift the car?

No. The standard ARC kit preserves factory ride height and ground clearance. It does not lift or lower the vehicle.

Q: Are my original Toyota coil springs retained?

Yes. The kit includes 2 front struts and 2 rear shock absorbers designed to work precisely with your original factory coil springs.

Q: Can any local mechanic install this kit?

Yes. Installation is a straightforward bolt-on process that matches OEM replacement procedures. Any certified multi-brand garage or alignment center can complete it in 2–3 hours.

Q: Is wheel alignment necessary after installation?

Yes. Computerized steering and wheel alignment is mandatory immediately following installation to ensure optimal tyre wear and vehicle stability.
