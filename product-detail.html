Description

Upgrade your vehicle's stability, steering response, and body control with ARC Suspension. Engineered specifically to handle uneven surfaces, road undulations, and highway curves, ARC shock absorbers provide controlled damping without sacrificing vehicle stability.

Benefits:

  • Increased Vehicle Stability: Firmer, tuned damping controls body float and keeps the chassis composed at highway speeds.
  • Reduced Body Roll & Sway: Limits lateral body sway during lane changes and cornering maneuvers.
  • Reduced Vibration on Steering: Dampens harsh road feedback through the chassis before reaching the driver.
  • Enhanced Braking Control: Reduces nose dive under firm braking, keeping the vehicle balanced.
  • Even Tyre Wear: Controlled vertical movement reduces uneven tyre wear across all axles.
  • Tuned for Indian Roads: Calibrated to withstand severe road conditions and sharp speed breakers.

How Do I Get ARC Suspension Installed?

  • Direct Replacement: Any qualified local garage, workshop, or tyre alignment centre can install this kit. If they can replace stock shock absorbers, they can install ARC dampers.
  • 100% Direct Bolt-On: Mounts directly onto OEM mounting brackets with zero chassis cutting, welding, or drilling.
  • Hardware Included: High-durability mounting bushings are included so installers have all necessary mounting components.
  • Technical Assistance: ARC provides direct technician-to-technician telephone support (+91 9922099292 / +91 9422047019) if any installer has questions.

Warranty:

Shock Absorbers: 40,000 km or 1 Year (whichever occurs first) manufacturer warranty against functional defects or damping failure.
